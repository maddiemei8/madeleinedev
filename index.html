About

Madeleine Ng is a software developer living on Vancouver Island. Growing up in the Pacific Northwest, she believes in technology that empowers communities through intentional and sustainable design. Madeleine currently attends Vancouver Island University as a computer science major.

Beyond her studies, Madeleine is working on applications related to web development, database systems, and the implementation of algorithms in software. madeleinedev.ca is landing spot for her past projects and works in progress.
